Bouncing Ball in a Cube

Pop all 3 targets! Pull from the ball and hit them on your 2nd or 3rd wall bounce.

Difficulty
Target size 2.44x

Pull from the ball at the center of the cube: stretch the line and you will see crosshairs on the surface that it will first hit.
Targets pop (and you get points) only on your 2nd or 3rd wall bounce (floor bounces count too). Miss and you lose one-fourth of the points of your last successful hit.
In easy mode, the points are given as the distance travelled before target hit. In medium mode, the distance is doubled. In hard mode, the distance it trippled.
Click (don't drag) anywhere after the ball's 2nd wall bounce to return it to the center instantly.
You may need to drag empty space (orbit the camera) and-or zoom or dezoom to get more pull force distance.
Adjusting the bounciness index of the walls may be helpful on some shots has well.
